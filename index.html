i.

The search

Dana Ben David, co-founder of Healthybud, needed beautiful emails that actually sold product. What she found were agencies and contractors who weren’t up to speed, and when she did find talent, the quotes started at $5,000 USD a month. Some wanted a share of revenue on top.

ii.

The breaking point

Then a contractor quit without notice, days before a deadline that mattered more than any other. She was left stranded, with emails to deliver and nobody to deliver them. That was the moment she decided she would never depend on someone else’s availability again.

iii.

The system

Determined to find a better way, she built one. By combining AI with Figma and Canva, she created a process that produces emails that look stunning and convert, reliably and quickly, with a human eye on every detail.

iv.

Now, for you

She now offers that same system to small businesses at a fraction of what they’d normally pay. No retainers that cost a fortune, no revenue share, no disappearing act. Start with three emails on us, and keep going only if you love them.
